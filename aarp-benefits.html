Organizing AARP's Member Benefits
I helped design and evolve AARP's Member Benefits Discovery Guide, a printed piece created to introduce members to everything their membership has to offer. The challenge was making a huge amount of information feel approachable, organized and, ideally, something you'd actually want to flip through.
I also created this insert to help members recognize all the benefits that come with AARP membership, updated quarterly to reflect the current season.
